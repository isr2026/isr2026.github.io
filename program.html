Plenary talks

Two plenary talks in the Conference Hall, on December 3 and December 4. Titles, speakers and abstracts are on the plenary talks page.

Parallel sessions

Oral sessions run in parallel in Room A and Room B. Session codes follow the pattern A11, B21 and so on.

Poster sessions

Two poster sessions are held in the Conference Hall, on December 2 and December 3. See presentation guidelines for poster size, and the poster page for submission.

Social program

A Welcome Party on December 2 and the Banquet on December 3, both in the Party Hall. Details will follow with the detailed program.
